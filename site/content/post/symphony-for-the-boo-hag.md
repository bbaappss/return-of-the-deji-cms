The Center for Science and the Imagination at ASU published my new short story [Symphony for the Boo Hag](https://csi.asu.edu/story/orchestra-for-public-good/#boohag). This emerged from a workshop with symphony musicians, conductors, and academics that asked the critical question - will the symphony orchestra survive? If so, who is it for? My story follows three kids who explore an abandoned house devastated by a hurricane. While they set about trying to make sense of their new reality, a spirit begins haunting the neighborhood. 

I enjoyed writing this one. [Read it here](https://csi.asu.edu/story/orchestra-for-public-good/#boohag). You can buy the entire collection of fiction and response essays [here](https://csi.asu.edu/books/soundsystems/).
