Could AI ever successfully manage a sports club? That is the topic of my [essay](https://issues.org/futuretensefiction/ai-alignment-sport-olukotun/) in Issues in Science and Technology. I respond to the delightful short story by Andres Martinez in which an AI manages a club to promotion in English soccer. The story raises questions not just about the ethics of AI itself but also what we want from global sports.

You can read my response essay [here](https://issues.org/futuretensefiction/ai-alignment-sport-olukotun/). You can read Andres Martinez's short story [here](https://issues.org/futuretensefiction/fiction-bonum-certamen-martinez/).
